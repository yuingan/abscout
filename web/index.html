AbScout

Sequence identity of an antibody's target between your species and the species antibodies are raised in or validated on (human, mouse, rat, rabbit), split into extracellular, transmembrane and intracellular regions, plus the epitope if you give it.

Pick from the list or type an NCBI taxonomy ID. The species must be in Ensembl Compara.
Epitope (optional)
From the antibody datasheet; AbScout finds where it sits.
UniProt numbering. If a peptide is given, the peptide is used.

Web version. Membrane topology comes from UniProt's curated annotation. A species
  without one is compared on whole-protein identity and the run notes say so; the
  source-code version predicts topology with
  TMbed and also runs many cases from a spreadsheet.

  
Everything runs in your browser. Queries go directly from your browser to Ensembl and
  UniProt; nothing is sent to or stored by AbScout.
